Molecular Targets

Key Therapeutic Targets

Our research focuses on proteins and signaling pathways that drive the key hallmarks of psoriasis and autoimmune skin disease.

S100 Proteins

S100A8 and S100A9 are key antimicrobial proteins overexpressed in psoriatic skin lesions. Our drug candidates target these proteins to disrupt the pro-inflammatory cascade that drives disease progression.

IL-6 Signaling

Interleukin-6 is a pleiotropic cytokine with a central role in systemic inflammation. Our local IL-6 modulation approach aims to achieve therapeutic efficacy while minimizing systemic side effects.

TNF-α Pathway

Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha is a master regulator of systemic inflammation. Our next-generation TNF-α inhibitors build on established mechanisms while improving selectivity and tolerability profiles.

Cell Signaling Inhibitors

Next-generation small molecule inhibitors targeting key cell signaling pathways involved in keratinocyte proliferation and immune cell activation — the fundamental drivers of psoriatic disease.
